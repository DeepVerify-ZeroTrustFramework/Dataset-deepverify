PROJECT TITLE:
  DeepVerify: Zero-Trust Interview Integrity Gateway

PURPOSE:
  We are collecting video and image data from volunteers to
  train and test our academic research project. The data will
  be used only for module testing and will NOT be published
  or shared outside the research team.

WHAT DATA IS COLLECTED:
  • Short video clips of your face (for rPPG and Behavioral)
  • Still images of a flat surface from your webcam (for PRNU)
  • Frame timestamp logs (for Jitter analysis)
  • Basic device and environment information

HOW IT WILL BE USED:
  • Used only for testing detection modules of DeepVerify
  • Stored securely on team Google Drive with restricted access
  • Identified only by a random Participant ID (not your name)
  • Not shared with any external party
  • Deleted after project submission (December 2025)

YOUR RIGHTS:
  • Participation is completely voluntary
  • You may withdraw at any time before submission
  • To request deletion of your data, contact the team with
    your Participant ID
